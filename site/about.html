About Northhaven

We make winter clothing for people who walk to the train in January.

Made carefully, in small runs

Our founder started Northhaven after a coat labelled "arctic" failed on a -18 °C commute. We test every piece on real walks before it gets a temperature rating.

Small runs mean we fix problems before the next batch, and we can explain who made what.

What you can count on

  • Materials listed on every product
  • 30-day returns on unworn items
  • Real customer reviews, never edited
  • A reply from a person within two working days

Winter Guide

What to wear by temperature, and answers to common questions.

Layering by temperature

  1. 0 to -10 °C: merino sweater, Commuter Down Coat, toque.
  2. -10 to -20 °C: add thermal leggings and gloves.
  3. Below -20 °C: swap to the Alpine Puffer and keep all layers.

Cotton holds sweat and gets cold fast. Wool blends and brushed linings keep warmth when damp.

Questions we hear most

How do I choose a size?

Measure your chest over a sweater. If you are between sizes, pick the larger one so the jacket fits over layers.

What is your return policy?

Return unworn items within 30 days for a refund. We cover return shipping on the first return.

Is the temperature rating a guarantee?

It is a guide for a brisk walk. Wind, damp and how you run cold or warm all change how a coat feels.

Do the gloves work with a phone?

Yes. The fingertips of the Touchscreen Knit Gloves are made for screens.
